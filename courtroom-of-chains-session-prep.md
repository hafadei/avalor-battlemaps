## Tier 2 Infernal Rift
### Paste-ready blocks, one per beat

## LFG / Mission Board Pitch

```
**RIFT: The Courtroom of Chains** — Tier 2
The merchant Varro Saltbearer has been dragged through a rift that opened in the market square. A devil's court is putting his soul on trial — and the rift is growing. Save Varro, close the rift, and crash the most dangerous courtroom in the Nine Hells.
**Reward:** 500 gp + Potion of Healing per PC
**Party:** 5 rifters, levels 6-7
```

## DM Background (read before session, don't paste)

The merchant Varro Saltbearer made a pact with the devil Malphas for commercial success. The contract's fine print contained a "devaluation clause" — if Varro ever sold goods below cost, his soul would be forfeit. During a famine, Varro sold grain at a loss to feed the hungry. The clause triggered. The rift opened, dragging Varro through. It manifests as an infernal courtroom where Malphas presides over the "trial" of Varro's soul.

The rift summons anyone with standing to serve as counsel — blood relations, contract holders, and champions. Mira (daughter) and Hestia (Guild factor) are pulled in by the rift's magic. The PCs are recognized as champions.

The Imp tampered with the contract. The original required "intent to devalue" — the Imp changed it to trigger on "any sale below cost." The original, untampered contract is hidden in the iron cage (west side of map). DC 15 Investigation to find it.

## BEAT 0 — OPENING NARRATIVE (paste to the Hall)
## ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

```
The Rifters' Hall is quiet tonight. A few of you sit by the hearth, nursing drinks, swapping stories of the last rift. The fire crackles. The keg is half-full. Outside, the city goes about its business — merchants closing shop, watchmen changing shifts, the distant clatter of cart wheels on cobblestone.

Then the hearth dies.

Not dims. *Dies*. The flames snuff out like a candle pinched between fingers. The Hall goes cold — a wrong cold, the kind that sinks into your bones and stays there. The flagstones in the center of the Hall groan, a deep and terrible sound, like the earth itself is in pain.

Then they crack.

A column of hellfire erupts from the split floor, roaring skyward, blackening the rafters. The heat hits you like a wall — dry, sulfurous, searing your lungs. Iron chains snake out of the fissure, rattling across the flagstones, coiling around table legs and bench posts like living things. The keg splits apart. Tankards scatter. The air smells of burning ink and old parchment.

Through the hellfire, you see it — not the cellar, not the foundation, but somewhere *else*. A vast chamber of black obsidian marble. Hellfire braziers casting orange light. Iron chains hanging from a ceiling lost to shadow. And at the far end, a judge's podium.

A courtroom. You're looking into a courtroom in Hell.

Two figures appear at the rift's edge — not walking, not climbing out, just *there*, as if the rift spat them out. A young woman in a flour-dusted dress, stumbling, catching herself on a chair. And behind her, a woman in Guild colors, ledger still in one hand, ink bottle in the other, eyes wide as saucers.

The young woman looks around wildly. "Where — where am I? I was in the shop. I was *in the shop*. Where's my father? He's on *trial*? For selling grain?!"

The Guild woman clutches her ledger to her chest. "I was at my desk. I was *at my desk*. What — what is this? Where — the Guild will pay. 500 gold. Plus healing potions. For whoever closes this. This rift — it pulled us here. It *pulled* us. Like it needed us. Like it needed witnesses."

The rift pulses. The chains rattle. Through the hellfire, a voice — deep, iron, patient — echoes up from the courtroom below:

"The Infernal Assize is in session. The defendant, Varro Saltbearer, stands accused of breach of contract. The penalty: forfeiture of soul. Let counsel approach."

The chains at the rift's edge uncoil toward you.

It's not a request.
```

### DM Notes for Beat 0
- **DC 12 Arcana or Religion:** This is an infernal contract rift — a soul is being claimed through legal process. The courtroom is a pocket of the Nine Hells bleeding through. It won't close until the "trial" concludes. The rift summoned everyone with standing — blood relations, contract holders, and champions — because infernal law requires the accused to have counsel.
- **DC 15 Insight on Mira:** She's telling the truth. She genuinely believes her father is innocent. She's terrified but resolute — she will not leave without him.
- **DC 15 Insight on Hestia:** The Merchants' Guild is more worried about property values than Varro's soul. But the payment is real, and she's genuinely shaken by being teleported across the city.
- Mira and Hestia may accompany the PCs or remain in the Hall — the rift allows them to choose, since they have standing but are not combatants.

## BEAT 1 — ENTERING THE COURTROOM (paste when PCs step through)
## ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

```
You step through the rift.

The sounds of the Rifters' Hall vanish behind you. You stand in the gallery of the infernal courtroom — elevated seating at the south end, looking down at the main floor. Black obsidian marble. Hellfire braziers casting orange light across iron chains that hang from a ceiling lost to shadow. The air is thick with sulfur and old parchment.

Below, on the main floor, a man in merchant's robes kneels in chains before a judge made of iron and malice. Chains coil from the judge's arms like serpents, clinking softly. Behind the judge, an elevated podium. To the east, a prosecution table covered in scrolls. To the west, an iron cage — a prisoner's box, empty except for old chains.

The judge looks up. His eyes are embers.
```

### Then paste — Judge Malphas speaks:

```
"Champions. The Assize recognizes your standing to advocate for the defendant. You may testify. You may argue. You may... fight." A chain rattles. "Or you may leave, and the trial proceeds without counsel. The defendant's soul will be forfeit either way. But at least it will be... *efficient.*"
```

### DM Notes for Beat 1
- The PCs can: **Leave** (failure), **Stay and testify** (triggers RP path), or **Attack** (triggers combat).
- If they stay, proceed to Beat 2.
- If they attack, skip to Beat 4.

## BEAT 2 — THE CHARGES (paste if PCs stay to listen)
## ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

```
The Prosecutor rises. She is tall, angular, her skin the color of burnished bronze. Barbed chains coil along her arms. She lifts a scroll from the prosecution table and reads in a voice like grinding stone:

"On the 14th of Marpenoth, the defendant Varro Saltbearer sold thirty barrels of grain at a price below cost, in direct violation of Article Seven, Section Three of his contract with the Infernal Exchange. The contract is clear. The penalty is forfeiture of soul. The evidence —" she gestures to the glowing contract on the prosecution table — "is irrefutable."

The contract glows with infernal script. You can read it from the gallery:

"Should the Signatory sell goods below the cost of acquisition, the Signatory's soul shall be forfeit to the Infernal Exchange, to be claimed at the discretion of the Court."

Varro, kneeling in chains, looks up at you with hollow eyes. "I didn't know what I was signing. A man in a nice suit came to my shop, offered me a loan, said I'd prosper. I just sold grain to people who were starving. I sold it for less than I paid. I didn't think anyone could *own* that. How is feeding people a crime?"
```

### DM Notes for Beat 2
- **DC 15 Investigation** on the contract: The ink on "below the cost of acquisition" is slightly darker — different handwriting. It's been altered.
- **DC 12 Arcana** on the contract: The infernal magic has a seam — like a patch sewn onto fabric.
- If PCs challenge the contract or want to argue, proceed to Beat 3.
- If PCs attack, skip to Beat 4.

## BEAT 3 — THE CHOICE (paste when PCs decide to act)
## ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

```
The judge leans forward. The chains around him go still.

"You wish to advocate for the defendant? Then choose your method. The Assize recognizes three forms of counsel:

You may **testify** — present evidence and argument before the court.
You may **investigate** — examine the courtroom, the contract, the records.
You may **fight** — challenge the court itself by force of arms.

Choose. The Assize is patient. But not indefinitely."
```

### DM Notes for Beat 3 — Three Paths

**PATH A — FIGHT:**
Attack the court. Skip to Beat 4.

**PATH B — ARGUE (RP):**
- DC 15 Persuasion to make a formal objection + the player must articulate why the contract is unjust
- Paste Prosecutor's objection:

```
"Objection! The witnesses are not licensed to practice infernal law! Their testimony is hearsay! Your Honor, I move to strike their entire argument from the record!"
```

- The Imp whispers misrepresentations to the judge:

```
A small voice hisses from behind the judge's podium — you catch it just barely. "Your Honor, I believe the witnesses are lying. Their body language suggests deception. They are clearly agents of chaos, sent to disrupt the lawful proceedings of this court."
```

- Without proof of forgery, the judge rules:

```
"The contract stands as written. The penalty is just. The Assize has heard your testimony and finds it... unconvincing."
```

- He's bound by infernal law — he can't overturn a valid contract without evidence of fraud. If PCs present the forged contract evidence (original from the cage), proceed to Beat 3C.

**PATH C — INVESTIGATE:**
- The Imp tampered with the contract. Original required Varro to sell "with intent to devalue" — the Imp changed it to trigger on "any sale below cost" (removing intent).
- **The original, untampered contract is hidden inside the iron cage** (west side of map). DC 15 Investigation to find it.
- **DC 12 Investigation** to spot the forgery on the prosecution table copy (different ink, slightly different handwriting).
- Presenting both copies to the judge + DC 15 Persuasion → proceed to Beat 3C.

### Beat 3C — FORGERY REVEALED (paste when PCs present evidence of tampering)

```
The judge takes both copies. His ember-eyes narrow. He holds them side by side, and the infernal script flickers — the forgery's magic guttering like a candle in wind.

"This... this is not the contract I ratified. The terms have been altered. *Altered.*" His chains go rigid. "Clerk. Approach the bench."
```

### Then paste — the Imp panics:

```
A small, winged creature darts out from behind the judge's podium — an imp, no taller than a cat, its tail lashing. It looks from the judge to the contracts to you.

"I — that wasn't — I was *improving* the contract! Making it more efficient! He was supposed to be *guilty*!"

It shape-shifts into a raven and bolts for the ceiling, squawking.
```

### Then paste — the judge's ruling:

```
The judge's chains lash out, snatching the raven from the air mid-flight. It shrieks, pinned.

"The Assize finds the contract *invalid*. The terms were altered without the knowledge or consent of the Signatory. The defendant is released. The clerk is... *remanded*."

The chains around Varro fall away. The courtroom trembles. The braziers flicker. The rift is closing.

"You may go," the judge says, and for the first time, his voice sounds almost... tired. "The Assize is adjourned."
```

### DM Notes for Beat 3C
- This is the non-combat victory. The rift closes, Varro is freed, the Imp is punished by the court.
- If the PCs attacked BEFORE finding the evidence, they can still find the forgery mid-combat and present it — the judge will halt the fight to examine it.

## TACTICAL NOTES (DM only — don't paste)
## ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

### Judge Malphas (Chain Devil) — The Anchor
- Stays on the podium (north) initially. Uses **Conjure Infernal Chain** (DC 15 Dex, 60ft) to pull PCs up to 30ft toward him — dragging them onto infernal sigils or away from healers.
- **Unnerving Gaze** (reaction, DC 15 Wis) frightens any PC who starts their turn within 30ft and can see him. This shuts down melee approaches.
- When a PC closes to melee, he uses Multiattack: 2 Chain attacks (grapple + restrain, DC 14 escape) + Conjure Infernal Chain.
- **Magic Resistance** makes him hard to control with spells.
- **When bloodied:** "You disrupt my court... you chain-breakers... you will find that justice has teeth."

### Prosecutor Vex'ahlia (Barbed Devil) — Artillery + Grappler
- Opens at range with **2× Hurl Flame** (150ft, +5, 5d6=17 fire each = 34/round). Targets spellcasters — especially the Bard (breaks concentration) and Warlock.
- If melee PCs approach, switches to Claws+Tail (10+14=24, grapples with claws DC 13).
- **Barbed Hide** deals 1d10 piercing to anyone grappling her — punishes the Barbarian/Monk if they try to pin her.
- **In combat:** "Let the fire of truth purify this court!"

### Bailiffs (2× Bearded Devils) — Chokepoint Controllers
- Start flanking the entrance (south). They intercept PCs as they enter.
- **Infernal Glaive** (reach 10ft) controls the chokepoint — they can hit PCs from 10ft away while PCs need to close.
- **Infernal Wound** (1d10 HP loss at start of each turn, DC 12 Con save or DC 12 Medicine to stanch) creates attrition. Devastating over multiple rounds.
- **Beard poison** prevents healing on the poisoned target — shuts down the Paladin's Lay on Hands and healing spells. Must cure the poison first (uses Lay on Hands, 5 HP per poison).
- Their glaive reach means they can attack from behind the bailiff and don't need to be adjacent.

### Gallery Guards (2× Spined Devils) — Harassers
- Start in the gallery (elevated, east/west). Fly over the battlefield.
- **Tail Spine** (ranged, +4, 20/80ft, 1d4+2 piercing + 1d6 fire) targets concentration checks on the Bard and Warlock.
- **Flyby** means they don't provoke opportunity attacks — they swoop in, sting, and leave.
- 45 HP each — a couple of good hits will drop them, but their mobility makes them hard to pin down.
- Counterplay: Ready an action to attack when they swoop in. Or use ranged attacks/spells to force them to take cover.

### Court Clerk (Imp) — Nuisance
- Starts invisible near the judge. Observes, relays tactical info to Malphas (flavor).
- Opportunistically stings vulnerable targets (6+7=13 poison).
- 21 HP — one good hit kills it. But it can shape-shift into a raven to flee.
- After attacking, it becomes visible (Invisibility breaks on attack). It must use an Action to re-cast Invisibility — so it can't attack and re-hide in the same turn.

## TERRAIN REFERENCE (DM only)

- **North (rows 1-3):** Elevated judge's podium, 10ft high. DC 12 Athletics to climb or difficult terrain. Chain Devil starts here.
- **Center (rows 7-13):** Infernal sigils carved into black marble. **Difficult terrain for non-fiends.** Any non-fiend starting their turn on a sigil takes **1d4 fire damage**.
- **East (cols N-T, rows 4-8):** Prosecution table with the infernal contract. Barbed Devil starts here.
- **West (cols A-G, rows 4-8):** Iron cage prison cell. Door can be opened/closed (action). Half cover inside. DC 15 Thieves' Tools to lock. **The original untampered contract is hidden inside** (DC 15 Investigation to find).
- **South (rows 16-20):** Grand arched entrance. PCs enter here. Two Bearded Devils flank the doorway.
- **Gallery seating (east/west, rows 4-8):** Elevated 10ft, provides cover. Spined Devils start here, flying.
- **Hellfire Braziers (4):** NE, NW, SE, SW corners. Bright light 10ft. Pushing a creature into a brazier: **2d6 fire, DC 12 Dex save** to avoid. Can be tipped (DC 12 Strength) to create a 10ft fire pool lasting 1 minute.

## DM SECRETS

- **The Imp's forgery** is the key to the non-combat path. Original contract required "intent to devalue" — Imp removed the intent clause. Original is in the iron cage (DC 15 Investigation).
- **Aura of Protection:** The L7 Paladin grants +CHA to all saves within 10ft. This significantly weakens the Chain Devil's DC 15 effects.
- **Barb/Monk is below power curve** — no Extra Attack from either class at Barb 4/Monk 2. Consider dropping a magic weapon in the iron cage alongside the contract.
- **All devils immune to fire** — Loravyre's fire spells won't work. Deliberate thematic challenge.
- **Scale down:** Drop the Imp (-200 XP) or one Spined Devil (-450 XP) if the party struggles.
- **Scale up:** Add 2× Lemures (CR 0, 10 XP each) as shambling gallery spectators for flavor/threat.
- **Mid-combat forgery:** PCs can still find the evidence in the iron cage during combat (DC 15 Investigation, takes an action to search). Presenting it to the judge halts the fight — he's bound by infernal law to examine it.
